## The short version

Tesla China did **not** rewrite the Model 3 / Model Y guide-price board in early September. What it did announce around **7 September 2026** was a **time-boxed inventory incentive**: cash on eligible stock cars if you order and take delivery by **30 September**.

That distinction matters. Headlines say “cuts.” The mechanism is closer to a quarter-end clearance lever — guide MSRP stays, effective transaction price moves for shoppers who accept inventory configuration and the deadline.

## What the public numbers say

Press-consistent package (dual-sourced across major English/Chinese auto business outlets):

- **Model 3 inventory:** about **5,000 RMB** cash incentive  
- **Model Y / Model Y L inventory:** about **10,000 RMB** cash incentive  
- Window: order + delivery by **30 Sep 2026**  
- **Guide prices reported unchanged**

Illustrative guide board (RMB, press-reported):

| Trim | Guide (RMB) | Approx incentive |
|------|-------------|------------------|
| Model 3 RWD | 235,500 | −5,000 inventory |
| Model 3 Long Range RWD | 259,500 | −5,000 inventory |
| Model Y RWD | 263,500 | −10,000 inventory |
| Model Y Long Range RWD | 288,500 | −10,000 inventory |
| Model Y L (6-seat) | 339,000 | −10,000 inventory |

Some outlets cite stacked floors (cash + financing / selected credits) near **~222,500 RMB** (Model 3) and **~253,500 RMB** (Model Y). Treat those as **effective** figures under stated stacks — not a permanent MSRP rewrite.

## So what (free)

If you only track “did Tesla cut the sticker?”, you will mis-read September. The competitive tell is **inventory + deadline + financing choreography** — the same grammar domestic rivals have been using in “Golden September” entitlement packages.

## Full read (members)

### Why this is volume defense, not a new product story

China’s passenger-car retail backdrop in early September reporting still looked soft on a YoY basis (CPCA-linked press). In that climate, Tesla’s core sedan/SUV pair is defending **throughput** against:

- Local BEVs that undercut on hardware features per RMB  
- EREVs that sell “range anxiety solved” without asking buyers to live on public charging  
- Entitlement stacks that make transaction math opaque on purpose  

An inventory sprint lets Tesla move metal **without** resetting the national guide board that anchors residual-value narratives and trim hierarchy.

### How to read it against Xiaomi / BYD / premium JV EVs

Xiaomi’s **Sky Nomad** EREV launch (7 Sep) put large-battery extended-range SUVs into the conversation at guides starting **209,900 RMB** — squarely in the conversation set that steals consideration from Model Y shoppers who were never pure BEV ideologues.

BYD’s dual-network ~**99,900 RMB** B-sedan shelf (Qin Max / Seal 06 family, mid-August launches still shaping September inventory) keeps pressure on the whole price ladder below Tesla.

BMW’s **iX3 LWB** pre-sale messaging from **269,900 RMB** shows premium JV product also willing to sit near Model Y money with a different brand promise.

Net: Tesla’s September move is rational **tactical** pricing. It is not, by itself, a strategic admission that the guide architecture collapsed.

### Method note

Figures above are compiled from public press reprints of Tesla China policy and contemporaneous coverage. For purchase or resale decisions, re-verify on Tesla China’s official channels — inventory eligibility and stacked credits vary by config and city.
