## The short version

**7 September 2026** put two stories on the same calendar: Tesla’s inventory sprint, and Xiaomi’s **Sky Nomad** (Pengcheng) EREV SUV family hitting hard launch prices. Around them, a wider **“Golden September”** wave of insurance, financing, and deposit-locked benefits — often **without** rewriting guide MSRPs.

If you still compare cars by sticker alone, you are reading last year’s war.

## Full read (members)

### Spec deltas that matter (as marketed)

Public English coverage clustered on:

- N70 Pro: ~**52 kWh LFP**, ~**351 km** CLTC EV / ~**1,351 km** combined  
- N70 Max: ~**76 kWh NMC**, ~**505 km** EV / ~**1,461 km** combined  
- N90 Max: ~**76 kWh**, ~**464 km** EV / ~**1,705 km** combined  
- 1.5T range extender; fast DC claims (e.g. 20–80% in ~18 minutes under stated conditions)  
- Xiaomi HAD / high-compute ADAS + LiDAR stacks on higher trims; HyperOS cabin

**Conflict to resolve before you cite drive layout:** some outlets describe N70 Pro as FWD, others as RWD. Apex Lane flags the conflict rather than inventing a winner — prefer Xiaomi’s official configurator capture in polish.

### Entitlement war grammar

Same week/month pattern across startups and others:

- Leapmotor autumn packages (five-figure RMB benefit envelopes in press)  
- Zeekr 8X insurance/financing stacks  
- Xiaomi SU7 / YU7 interest or insurance subsidies  
- XPeng MONA M03 deposit-locked benefits  

**Analytical rule:** benefit ≠ guide cut. Transaction price can move 10–20k while the brochure MSRP stays “stable” for residual and headline politics.

### Shelf pressure below and beside

- BYD **Qin Max / Seal 06** family still anchors the ~**99,900 RMB** smart-sedan shelf from mid-August launches  
- BMW **iX3 LWB** pre-sale from **269,900 RMB** crowds the premium-SUV conversation Tesla and Xiaomi both want  
- Geely Galaxy **Cruiser 700** pre-sales flagged for **15 Sep** with price TBD at pack close — watch list, not pricing board

### So what for readers who pay

Sky Nomad is not “another crossover.” It is Xiaomi proving it can enter **EREV family SUV** with aggressive guides and a software brand. Combined with entitlement season, September’s real scoreboard is **effective offer math** — and that scoreboard is exactly what free headlines flatten.
